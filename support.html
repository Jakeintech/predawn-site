Help with Predawn

Get in touch

Email info@jakeawilliams.com. It helps to include your device, your iOS version, the place and date you were planning, and what you expected to happen. A person reads every message.

Getting started

What does Predawn need to work?
An iPhone or iPad on iOS 26 or later. Location helps for tonight's light where you are; without it, search for a place or drop a pin.
Do I need an account?
No. There is nothing to sign up for. Your plans stay on your device.

Tonight and the forecast

What do Go, Check and No mean?
They answer whether tonight is worth going out for the night sky. The score combines how clear the forecast is during full darkness, whether the moon is down or thin, and how long it is truly dark. Tap the verdict for the reasons and the cloud by hour.
It says the forecast didn't load.
Forecasts come from Apple Weather and need a connection. Sun and moon times always work offline. Open the app again when you are back online.
Why do the times differ from another app by a minute?
Predawn uses the standard definitions: sunrise and sunset when the top of the sun touches the horizon, and twilights by the sun's centre. Golden hour runs from 6° above to 4° below the horizon, blue hour from 4° to 6° below. Other apps choose other angles.

Planning

How do I line up the moon with a landmark?
Tap Find dates. Choose the sun or the moon, rising or setting, the landmark you want it behind, and where you can stand. Predawn searches the next year and lists every match, best light first, with how far to step to centre it.
What does "stand 35 ft to the right" mean?
On that date the moon reaches the landmark a little to one side. Move that far, sideways to the line between you and the landmark, and it sits dead centre.
How does line of sight work?
It checks the ground between you and the horizon in the direction of the sun or moon and tells you the margin, and the minute it clears anything in the way. Terrain comes from Copernicus DEM, downloaded for the area you plan in and kept for use offline. If it can't load, Predawn says so and treats both ends as level ground; you can also enter the height of what's in front of you.
Does the camera view work everywhere?
It needs a device with a camera and works best after a short calibration: point at the sun or moon to lock the compass. Without a camera, use Scout's 3D view instead.

Alerts and the field

Which alerts will I get?
For each saved plan: a forecast re-check 48 and 3 hours before (it says if the outlook changed), a leave-now reminder based on drive and walk time, and an optional wake alarm. Change them in Settings > Alerts.
How do I turn on Red night?
From Settings, the Control Center control, or let it turn on by itself after dark.
Will my plan work with no signal?
Yes. Times, positions, line of sight and the timeline are saved with the plan. Maps you haven't viewed may not load offline.

Predawn Pro

What does Pro add?
Milky Way core, terrain shadow and tide layers on the map, forecast alerts and cloud by height, more than one Field pack, NOAA tide predictions for US coasts, polar alignment, star trails, time-lapse and the Apple Watch app.
How do I restore a purchase?
Open the Pro screen from Settings and tap Restore Purchases. Use the same Apple Account you bought with.
How do I cancel the yearly plan?
In Settings > [your name] > Subscriptions on your iPhone, at least 24 hours before it renews.
