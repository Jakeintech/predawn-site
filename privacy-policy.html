Privacy Policy

Last updated November 16, 2026

The short version

  • Predawn has no accounts, no ads and no analytics or tracking software.
  • Your plans, places, gear and photos stay on your device.
  • Your location is used on your device. Its coordinates are sent to Apple to fetch the weather forecast, maps and place search. They are not sent to us.
  • We do not run a server that receives your data, and we do not sell or share personal information.

1. Who we are

Predawn is a sun and moon photo planner for iPhone and iPad made by Jake Williams ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the app handles, where it goes and the choices you have. Questions: info@jakeawilliams.com.

3. Location

With your permission, Predawn uses your location to work out tonight's light, the sun and the moon where you are, and to request the forecast for that spot. You can refuse or withdraw permission at any time in Settings > Privacy & Security > Location Services > Predawn. Without it you can still choose any place by searching or dropping a pin.

4. Weather, maps, terrain, landmark heights and tides

Forecasts come from Apple Weather (WeatherKit). Each request contains the coordinates of your location or a place you chose, and nothing that identifies you to us. Maps, place search, 3D views and Look Around use Apple Maps. Apple handles these requests under its own privacy policy. Tide predictions (Pro) come from NOAA's public tide service: Predawn downloads NOAA's list of stations, picks the nearest one on your device, and asks NOAA only for that station's number and the dates you are looking at, never your location. All sun and moon calculations, line of sight, terrain shadows and tonight's verdict are worked out on your device.

Hills and ridges come from Copernicus DEM, a free elevation model published by the European Union and ESA and hosted by Amazon Web Services. When you plan with a spot and a landmark, the app downloads the parts of the one-degree map squares they sit in. A request names the map square (for example "N39 W105") and a byte range, nothing that identifies you or your exact position; as with any download, Amazon sees the network address the request comes from. Amazon handles these requests under its own privacy notice. Downloaded terrain is kept on your device (not backed up) so plans work without signal.

Landmark heights come from OpenStreetMap and Wikidata. When you make a building or landmark the subject of a plan and Predawn doesn’t know its height, it asks the public OpenStreetMap Overpass service (overpass-api.de) for buildings within about 150 m of the landmark’s coordinates, and if none has a height, asks the Wikidata Query Service (query.wikidata.org, run by the Wikimedia Foundation) the same about tagged heights nearby. The request contains only those coordinates and the app’s name and version; picking the right building happens on your device. Each landmark is looked up once, and a height you type yourself is never replaced. As with any download, these services see the network address the request comes from and handle it under their own privacy policies.

5. Notifications and alarms

Forecast re-checks, leave-now reminders and wake alarms are scheduled on your device. No push notification server is involved, and we do not know whether an alert was delivered or opened.

6. Purchases

Predawn Pro is sold by Apple as a yearly subscription or a one-time purchase. Apple processes the payment and tells the app on your device whether Pro is unlocked. We do not see your card number, billing address or Apple Account.

7. What we do not do

  • No accounts or sign-in.
  • No advertising, and no advertising identifier.
  • No third-party analytics, tracking or crash-reporting software.
  • No sale or sharing of personal information.

8. Keeping and deleting your data

Your plans, places, gear and settings stay on your device until you delete them. You can delete any plan in the app. Deleting Predawn removes everything it stored. Because we hold none of your data, there is nothing for us to delete on our side.

9. Children

Predawn is rated 4+ and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has sent us personal information by email, contact us and we will delete it.

10. Your rights

Depending on where you live, laws such as the GDPR or the CCPA give you rights to access, correct, delete or restrict the use of your personal data. Because your data is stored on your own device, you can do these things directly in the app. If you have a request we can help with, write to info@jakeawilliams.com.

11. Security

Predawn relies on the protections built into iOS: its data sits inside the app's sandbox, protected by your passcode and the device's encryption, and every network request uses HTTPS.

12. Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date at the top of this page. For a significant change we will also say so in the app.
