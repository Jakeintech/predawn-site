2. What Predawn does

Predawn is a sun and moon photo planner. It includes:

  • Tonight. Light times, the moon and a verdict for the night sky based on Apple Weather data.
  • Planning. Sun and moon positions on a map, Find dates for alignments with a subject, line of sight, a 3D scout view and a camera view.
  • Saved plans. Timelines, pack lists, alerts and alarms, kept on your device.
  • Tools. Photography calculators and polar alignment.
  • Widgets and more. Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets, a Live Activity, Siri and Control Center controls.
